04How the analysis is produced

Analysis is calculated from candle data with fixed rules (for example ICT, SMC, VSA and candlestick patterns), combined across timeframes, and sometimes checked with AI. Different traders can read the same chart differently, and rules cannot see news, spreads, slippage or sudden events. Levels estimated from an uploaded picture can be off. Always confirm levels on your own chart and with your broker before acting.

05Data can be wrong or late

Prices, volume, news and calendar data come from third parties. They may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate, and may differ from your broker’s prices. Forex and Deriv synthetic indices have no central market volume, so VSA may use a related futures contract or spread as a proxy and says so on screen. Do not trade only on what you see here.

06Risk management is your job

Always use a stop loss, size positions to a small share of your account, and understand leverage. The Risk & TP Planner, lot calculator and checklist are aids, not guarantees; check their numbers against your broker’s contract specifications.

07Paper Trade is not real trading

Paper Trade uses virtual money. Results there are not real and usually look better than live trading because they ignore emotion, slippage, liquidity, rejected orders and costs.
