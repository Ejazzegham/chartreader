Effective date: November 7, 2026  ·  Operated by Bahu Technology

Short version: we collect what is needed to run your account, subscription, tools and support; we do not sell your data; we do not store your IP address on your record; and you can delete your account in the app.

03How we use your information

  • To provide, secure and improve the Service and its tools.
  • To run your trial and subscription and to verify and approve payments.
  • To send account emails (confirm sign-up, reset password, change email), notifications and alerts you asked for, and support replies.
  • To moderate the Community, enforce our Terms and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To keep records and comply with legal obligations.

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running and protecting the Service, your consent where you give it (for example notifications or choosing to upload an image), and legal obligations.

04AI features

When you run an AI analysis of a chart image, or use chat translation, the image or text is sent through our server to Google’s Gemini AI service to produce the result. Do not upload images or write messages that contain information you do not want processed this way. Analysis levels estimated from a picture can be wrong.

05Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers who help us run Chart Reader, and only what they need:

  • Google / Firebase – sign-in (email and Google), account records, and AI (Gemini).
  • Expo push service, Google FCM and Apple APNs – delivering push notifications.
  • Email delivery provider – sending account emails (for example Resend or Brevo).
  • Hosting and network providers – such as Vercel, Netlify and Cloudflare – to serve the site and our server.
  • IP geolocation services (ipwho.is, ipapi.co) – to find the country and city at signup.
  • Market-data and widget providers – such as TradingView, Binance, Yahoo Finance, Deriv and an economic-calendar feed. Your browser or our server contacts them to load prices and charts, and they may receive your IP address. TradingView widgets are subject to TradingView’s own privacy policy.
  • Blockchain explorers – to check payment transactions. Blockchain data is public.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights and users, or in a business transfer. Other Community members see only your first name, last initial, country and what you post.

06Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage (and secure storage in the app) to keep you signed in, remember your settings, PIN counters and device ID, and count visits. These are needed for the Service to work. We do not use advertising cookies. Third-party widgets such as TradingView may set their own cookies; you can block cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the Service may stop working.

07How long we keep data

  • Account, trading data and cloud backup: while your account exists. When you delete your account these are deleted, except what we must keep by law or for fraud prevention.
  • Payment records (TxID, wallet, amount): kept as long as needed for accounting, dispute handling and to stop duplicate claims.
  • Support chats and attachments: kept while needed to help you and to keep a record of support.
  • Community posts: until removed by you, by moderators, or when the account is deleted.
  • Visit counter: only one-way hashes, no personal data.

08Your choices and rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app and delete your account in Profile → Delete account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict the use of your data, to object to some processing, and to withdraw consent. Email bahutechnology@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can turn off notifications in your device settings, and revoke photo or camera access at any time. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

09Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), verified sign-in tokens, random file names for uploads, rate limits and access controls. The app PIN and biometric lock stay on your device. No system is 100% secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your devices safe.

10International transfers

Our providers operate in several countries, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live. We use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

11Children

Chart Reader is for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will change the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email.
